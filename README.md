## 🕹️ About

Parisian Bistro Simulator is a casual management and strategy game by Quark Studios, published by Quark Studios and Gamersky Games. Set in the heart of Paris, it puts you in charge of a growing bistro surrounded by Haussmannian architecture and views of the Eiffel Tower. Build seasonal menus, manage supplies and finances, hire and train staff, serve customers, and expand from a small restaurant into a Parisian institution. The game supports solo play and online co-op for up to eight players, with hands-on service, kitchen minigames, restaurant customization, and rooftop expansion.

A relaxed yet detailed restaurant-management experience for players who enjoy simulation, customization, hospitality, and cooperative teamwork.

## ✨ Features

- 💶 **Financial Strategy** — Balance sales, salaries, rent, electricity, taxes, and other operating expenses.
- 🗼 **Heart of Paris** — Run a bistro in an atmospheric Haussmannian setting beneath the Eiffel Tower.
- 🤝 **Eight-Player Co-op** — Work together online as a team and divide responsibilities across the restaurant.
- 📦 **Supply Planning** — Manage ingredients, inventory, suppliers, ordering thresholds, and daily restaurant demands.
- 🍽️ **Menu Creation** — Design seasonal menus featuring French-inspired dishes, drinks, breakfasts, and cocktails.
- 👥 **Staff Management** — Hire, train, organize, and develop employees with distinct skills, traits, and specialties.
- 🎧 **Rooftop Nights** — Serve cocktails, hire DJs, and create a lively rooftop destination for evening guests.
- 🍳 **Kitchen Workstations** — Use specialty workstations and interactive minigames to improve kitchen productivity.
- 🏗️ **Restaurant Expansion** — Unlock new floors, a terrace, and a rooftop as the bistro grows.
